    • Other Information

    Flash Point Temperature (K)

    The minimum temperature at which a material can vaporize and ignite in air. Used to assess fire hazard and handling safety requirements.

    Safety Classification

  • The hazard index (HI) is with respect to the physical hazard of the system and does not include the health hazard.

  • A standardized rating system (e.g., GHS and NFPA classification) is used as the basis for this classification.

  • The lower the HI value, the safer the PCM
